Show results from at least two measured runs. A claim without a number is a plan, not a result.

Full marks look like this. Baseline profile: phases or operators with their shares, the raw profiler output committed. Changes by measured impact: a log ordered by effect, each change measured after the previous one, null results kept. Quality re-evaluation: a paired comparison overall and per group with intervals against a floor stated in advance. Benchmark method: warm-up, repetitions, a fixed input file, percentiles with intervals, the machine. Reproducibility: a fresh clone and the README's commands reproduce the table. The capstone rubric is 60% of the course grade, the live defence 20%, the supervised checks 20%.

Peer feedback. After each talk, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion.

Keep the eight minutes strict: the timer on the presenting slide is for that. The re-run happens inside the eight minutes; if the laptop fails, play the recorded backup and note it.

The four minutes of questions

The assessor proposes a change; you predict its effect, then measure it

1 · Propose"Use one thread", "batch 8", "the 8-bit file", "a longer prompt"
→
2 · Predictdirection and rough size, with the reason: which limit binds
→
3 · Measurere-run the benchmark with the change, live
→
4 · Explainwhy the number matched the prediction, or why not

This is the authenticity check of the defence, from the course's assessment: the participant predicts the effect of a change the assessor proposes and then measures it. Choose a change the benchmark script supports without new code. A wrong prediction explained well scores better than a right one that cannot be explained.

What reviewers flag most often

Five gaps to close before you present

  • A speed-up without the machine, threads and batch it was measured with.
  • A median from one run, without repetitions or an interval.
  • Quality checked overall but not per group.
  • Changes listed in the order they were tried, not by measured impact.
  • A README whose commands do not reproduce the table from a fresh clone.
